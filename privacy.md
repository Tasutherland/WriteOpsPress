      <p style="color: var(--color-text-muted); font-size: var(--text-sm);">Last updated: October 15, 2026</p>

      <h2>1. What We Collect</h2>
      <p>The website does not operate accounts, checkout, or email signup forms. We may receive information only when you choose to contact us directly:</p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Contact details and message content</strong> — when you send us an email.</li>
        <li><strong>Optional Google Analytics 4</strong> — only after you accept analytics, we measure visits, page views, traffic sources, browser/device information, approximate location, and clicks to book retailers. Click records include the book title, public Work ID where available, author, ebook or audiobook format, retailer, storefront region, and originating page. These are measures of interest, not confirmed purchases.</li>
      </ul>
      <p>We do <strong>not</strong> collect payment information or ask readers to create an account on this site.</p>

      <h2>2. How We Use It</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>To respond to messages you send us.</li>
        <li>To improve the site based on aggregate traffic patterns.</li>
        <li>We do not sell or rent your personal information for marketing purposes.</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>4. Cookies</h2>
      <p>Optional analytics is off by default for visitors in every country, including Canada, the UK, and the EU/EEA. If you reject analytics or make no choice, we do not load the Google Analytics tag or send analytics events to Google. Book links and the rest of the site remain available.</p>
      <p>If you accept, Google Analytics uses first-party <code>_ga</code> and <code>_ga_*</code> cookies to distinguish browsers and sessions. Our tag limits these cookies to 180 days without renewal on every visit. We store your analytics choice in this browser's local storage for up to 180 days, then ask again. This preference storage is used to remember your choice, not to track browsing.</p>
      <p>You can change your choice at any time using <strong>Analytics settings</strong> in the footer. Rejecting after acceptance stops further collection and removes analytics cookies accessible to this site. Withdrawal does not erase data previously collected with your consent. If browser storage is blocked, your choice may apply only to the current page.</p>
      <p>Third-party services such as GitHub Pages and Google Fonts may still process standard request data according to their own policies.</p>

      <p>You have the right to:</p>

      </ul>
      <p>Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, request data portability, withdraw consent, or complain to your privacy regulator. This includes rights under applicable Canadian, EU/EEA, UK, and other privacy laws. Contact us with requests. We do not require analytics consent to use this site.</p>

      <h2>6. Data Retention</h2>
      <p>We retain direct correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to answer the request, maintain business records, or meet legal obligations.</p>
      <p>Analytics user-level and event-level data is subject to the retention setting in our GA4 property; aggregate reporting can be retained longer. The cookie and preference durations above concern your browser and are separate from Google's reporting retention. We use analytics to assess traffic and reader interest, not to identify individual readers.</p>

      <h2>7. Children</h2>
      <p>This site is intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has sent us personal information, please contact us immediately.</p>

      <h2>8. Changes to This Policy</h2>
      <p>We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Analytics remains optional; continued browsing is not consent to analytics.</p>
